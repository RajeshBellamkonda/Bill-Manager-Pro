Can't see notifications?

Here's how to enable notifications on different operating systems.

Windows 10 / 11
  • Check global notifications setting is on: Go to Start → Settings → System → Notifications & actions → Show app notifications.
  • Allow notifications for your browser: In the same place, scroll down to find a list of apps. Make sure the entry for your browser is set to "On" and "Show notification banners" is also on.
  • Turn off Focus Assist: Go to Start → Settings → System → Focus assist. Turn it off, or set to "Priority Only" and add your browser to the accepted list.
macOS
  • Turn off Do Not Disturb: System Settings → Focus → Do Not Disturb.
  • Enable notifications for browser: System Settings → Notifications. Find your browser and set alert style to "Alerts" or "Banners".
Linux
  • Ensure your desktop environment's notification daemon is running.
  • Check that your browser is not blocked in the system notification settings.
Android
  • Enable notifications for your browser: Settings → Apps & notifications → See all apps. Find your browser and make sure "Show notifications" is on.
iOS
  • Turn off Do Not Disturb: Settings → Do Not Disturb.
  • Enable notifications for browser: Settings → Notifications. Find your browser and enable "Allow Notifications".
ℹ️ This page will never serve you notifications except when you click the "Send Notification" button.
